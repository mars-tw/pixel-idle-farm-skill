## 第 1 題：這個 App 做什麼

中文：

陽光農場是單人農場遊戲。田上有一道會移動的光，落下前 8 秒會先亮預告圈。玩家拿祖母留下的手鏡把光折到自己選的那一格，在光裡種、澆、收，接到的陽光存進蓄光瓶。存下來的陽光可以催熟一格作物，或拿去曬架把收成曬成乾貨。作物分喜光、半日照、耐陰三種，該不該照光要自己判斷。收成後回農舍灶台顧火做料理，再把料理分給鎮上四位鄰居，推進五章主線。開場對鎮長、班伯、蘿拉的回答各留下一個一直有效的差異，他們之後會回頭提起。在光裡做到的事會蓋進「追光手冊」，集滿 3、6、10、15 枚印章各領一份獎勵，其中三份是永久加成。

遊戲不用註冊帳號，沒有廣告，沒有內購，進度存在裝置上。

English:

Sunny Farm is a single-player farming game. A beam of sunlight moves across the field, and a ring marks where it will land eight seconds in advance. The player uses a hand mirror to redirect the beam to a plot of their choice, then plants, waters or harvests inside the light to collect sunlight into a jar. Stored sunlight can ripen a plot instantly or power a drying rack that turns crops into dried goods. Crops have three light traits (sun-loving, half-sun, shade-tolerant), so the player has to decide which plot should receive each beam. After harvest the player tends a stove fire to cook, shares meals with four neighbours, and progresses through a five-chapter story. The answers the player gives early on to three townspeople (the mayor, the old farmer and the merchant) each leave a lasting difference on the farm, and each of them refers back to that answer later. What the player achieves in the light is stamped into a sun-chasing handbook; collecting 3, 6, 10 and 15 stamps each unlocks a reward, three of which are permanent upgrades.

The app requires no account, shows no advertising, has no in-app purchases, and stores progress on the device.

English:

## 第 3 題：解決什麼需求

中文：

給玩家一個可以離線玩、每次幾分鐘就有進展的農場。和常見農場遊戲的「種下去等時間到」相比，這裡每一道光都要做一次決定：折給哪一格、哪種作物該照、陽光留著催熟還是拿去曬。我們沒有宣稱這是市場上唯一的做法。

English:

It offers an offline farm that gives progress in sessions of a few minutes. Instead of only planting and waiting for a timer, each beam of light asks for a decision: which plot gets it, which crop benefits from it, and whether to spend the stored sunlight on ripening or drying. We do not claim this is unique in the market.

## 第 8 題：有沒有用第三方模板或 App 產生服務

中文：

沒有買現成模板，也沒有用 App 產生服務。遊戲用原生 HTML、CSS、JavaScript 寫成，沒有用遊戲引擎。用到的第三方與工具：

- Capacitor（開源）負責把網頁包成 App。
- 寫程式時用了 AI 程式助理（OpenAI Codex、Anthropic Claude），由開發者指定需求、檢查與測試。
- 美術主要用 AI 圖像工具產生（OpenAI 的圖像生成工具，以及 MUSE 的圖像生成），再用專案裡的腳本去背、裁切、定色盤後才放進遊戲。背景音樂用 ACE-Step 在本機產生。每一批的工具、提示詞與檔案雜湊記在 [CREDITS.md](../../CREDITS.md) 和各素材資料夾的 manifest。
- 操作音效是程式即時合成的，沒有音效檔。

- 沒有用第三方素材包。11 月 11 日查過 `assets/` 的全部資料夾、整個 repo 的檔名與 git 歷史，沒有 Kenney、OpenGameArt 或其他素材包的檔案。`CREDITS.md` 之前有三句提到這兩個名字，是沿用的套話，已經刪掉，查核方式寫在該文件的「第三方素材包查核」一節。

English:

No purchased template and no app-generation service. The game is written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript without a game engine. Third-party components and tools:

- Capacitor (open source) packages the web game as an app.
- AI coding assistants (OpenAI Codex, Anthropic Claude) were used while writing the code, under the developer's direction, review and testing.
- Most artwork was produced with AI image tools (OpenAI image generation and MUSE image generation) and then cut out, cropped and palette-reduced by scripts in the project before use. The background music was generated locally with ACE-Step. Tools, prompts and file hashes are recorded per batch in the project's credits and asset manifests.
- Sound effects are synthesised at runtime; there are no sound files.

- No third-party asset packs. On 11 November we checked every folder under `assets/`, all file names in the repository and the git history, and found no files from Kenney, OpenGameArt or any other asset pack.
