## 第 1 題：這個 App 做什麼、替使用者解決什麼問題

Apple 要求：詳細說明，不可以拿商店上的行銷描述當答案。

中文：

陽光農場是單人農場遊戲。田上有一道會移動的光，落下前 8 秒會先亮預告圈。玩家拿祖母留下的手鏡把光折到自己選的那一格，在光裡種、澆、收，接到的陽光存進蓄光瓶。存下來的陽光可以催熟一格作物，或拿去曬架把收成曬成乾貨。作物分喜光、半日照、耐陰三種，該不該照光要自己判斷。收成後回農舍灶台顧火做料理，再把料理分給鎮上四位鄰居，推進五章主線。開場對鎮長、班伯、蘿拉的回答各留下一個一直有效的差異，他們之後會回頭提起。在光裡做到的事會蓋進「追光手冊」，集滿 3、6、10、15 枚印章各領一份獎勵，其中三份是永久加成。

它是遊戲，不是工具，要解決的事情很單純：讓人可以玩幾分鐘農場就有進展，而且不必註冊帳號、不必連線、不會被廣告或內購打斷。遊戲不用註冊帳號，沒有廣告，沒有內購，進度存在裝置上。

English:

Sunny Farm is a single-player farming game. A beam of sunlight moves across the field, and a ring marks where it will land eight seconds in advance. The player uses a hand mirror to redirect the beam to a plot of their choice, then plants, waters or harvests inside the light to collect sunlight into a jar. Stored sunlight can ripen a plot instantly or power a drying rack that turns crops into dried goods. Crops have three light traits (sun-loving, half-sun, shade-tolerant), so the player has to decide which plot should receive each beam. After harvest the player tends a stove fire to cook, shares meals with four neighbours, and progresses through a five-chapter story. The answers the player gives early on to three townspeople (the mayor, the old farmer and the merchant) each leave a lasting difference on the farm, and each of them refers back to that answer later. What the player achieves in the light is stamped into a sun-chasing handbook; collecting 3, 6, 10 and 15 stamps each unlocks a reward, three of which are permanent upgrades.

It is a game rather than a utility, and the problem it addresses is a simple one: a farm that gives progress in a few minutes of play without requiring an account or a connection, and without being interrupted by advertising or purchases. The app requires no account, shows no advertising, has no in-app purchases, and stores progress on the device.

喜歡慢慢種田、每次玩幾分鐘、想看一點鄉村故事的休閒玩家，也適合家人一起玩。介面全是繁體中文，年紀小、還不太會讀字的玩家需要家長在旁邊幫忙。遊戲沒有戰鬥、沒有倒數壓力，也不要求連續登入。這和我們 10 月的回覆是同一個說法：家庭取向的單人農場遊戲，年紀小的玩家由家長陪同。

## 第 3 題：補上市場的哪個缺口

Apple 要求：說明現有 App 沒做到或做得比較差的地方，要有實質內容。

中文：

我們看到的農場遊戲，常見的做法是種下去、等時間到、收成。陽光農場把等待換成判斷，每一道光都要做一次決定：折給哪一格、哪種作物該照、陽光留著催熟還是拿去曬。作物的三種日照性格讓同一道光對不同的田有不同的結果，玩家得自己安排。

另外三件事也是刻意做的：整個遊戲沒有廣告、沒有內購、不用帳號；不連線也能玩；介面是繁體中文。

照實說，我們沒有做過完整的市場調查，不會說別的 App 都沒有類似的做法。我們能說的是：折光、三種日照性格、蓄光瓶的取捨這套核心玩法是為這款遊戲設計的，不是把現成的農場遊戲換一層外觀。

English:

The common pattern in the farming games we are aware of is to plant, wait for a timer and harvest. Sunny Farm replaces the waiting with a decision. Each beam of light asks the player to choose which plot gets it, which crop benefits from it, and whether the stored sunlight is better spent on ripening or on drying. Because crops have three different light traits, the same beam has a different result on different plots, and the player has to plan around that.

Three further choices were deliberate: the whole game has no advertising, no in-app purchases and no account; it is playable offline; and it is written in Traditional Chinese.

To be direct, we have not carried out a formal market survey and we do not claim that no other app does anything similar. What we can state is that the core loop here (redirecting light, three light traits, and the trade-off in spending stored sunlight) was designed for this game and is not an existing farming game with a new surface.
